Public address (base URI)

The address people and MCP clients reach this console at, e.g. https://ops.example.com/ramen. When set, every link the console generates goes through it — pages, forms, redirects, email links, OAuth discovery and the address handed to workers — so the console can sit behind a proxy that forwards <base URI>/… to its root. Empty uses RAMEN_PUBLIC_URL ({{ public_url_env or 'unset' }}), then the request's own address. A wrong value breaks every link: clear it with PUT /api/v1/config/base-uri {"base_uri": ""} at the console's root.

OAuth role mapping

Rules per sign-in provider: a value of the provider's role claim (an AD group, an IdP role) maps to a Ramen role and to the MCP groups the person may reach. Once a claim is named, the rules are applied on every login — role and groups set by hand are replaced, a person whose values match no rule becomes a viewer without groups, and several matches take the highest role and the union of groups. The bootstrap super admin ({{ auth.admin_email or 'RAMEN_ADMIN_EMAIL' }}) is never demoted. People signed in this way reach workers through the OAuth clients below instead of an agent key.

Blocked regions

Cloud regions no zone may be created in or deployed to — data-residency or cost rules.
