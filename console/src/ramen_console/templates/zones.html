Zones

A zone serves the groups it lists: binding a group creates its worker record for the zone, unbinding tears the group's deployment in the zone down (a zone an environment still uses cannot be unbound). Environments pick from the zones bound to their group.

Workers per group

One row per bound group × zone. A worker is one pod: the Rust node (gRPC + Streamable HTTP, auth, limits) plus a Python sidecar that runs the group's tools. The sidecar starts on the first call and is stopped after RAMEN_SIDECAR_IDLE_SECS (default 300) without calls, then starts again on the next one — "sidecar stopped" in a log is idle, not broken; set a very large value on the deploy to keep it warm. The live cell refreshes every 15 s: one entry per worker with its load (low / even / high, calls in flight over total) and the canary when one is running.
